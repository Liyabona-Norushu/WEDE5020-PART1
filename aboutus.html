Who We Are?

Sip & Sack Co. was founded in 2024 with a straightforward concept: commonplace objects like canvas tote bags, stainless-steel tumblers, and enamel badges should be as distinctive as the individuals who use them.

About Sip & Sack Co.

Our Story

When Sip & Sack Co. was first established in 2024, it was a modest, hands-on store that sold bright enamel badges, sturdy canvas tote bags, and personalized stainless-steel tumblers. Initially, we handled each purchase by ourselves via emails, word-of-mouth, and social media posts. Tracking orders and payments by hand proved too challenging as our products gained popularity outside of our local area. We opened our official online store to give our consumers in South Africa a quicker, safer, and simpler purchase experience. Custom design previews, safe payments, and automated ordering are all now available on our new website.
