# Changelog - Sip & Sack Co. Website

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-13

### Added
* **Homepage (`index.html`):**
  * Created the main landing page with a hero banner introduces Sip & Sack Co. lifestyle accessories[cite: 10].
  * Featured initial product showcases including plastic tumbler cups and stainless-steel tumblers[cite: 10].
  * Added brand overview highlighting delivery across Motherwell, Gqeberha, and the Eastern Cape[cite: 10].

* **About Us Page (`aboutus.html`):**
  * Added company history detailing business launch in 2024 and transition from manual social media orders to an official website[cite: 7].
  * Defined company Mission and Vision statements focused on high-quality personalized goods across South Africa[cite: 7].
  * Outlined target market details for students, young professionals, small businesses, and event planners[cite: 7].

**Services Page (`services.html`):**
  * Created showcase section for custom product options including travel mugs, assorted pint cups, and multi-color cold cups[cite: 11].
  * Added images and descriptions for available product styles and colors (`tumbler-vs-travel-mugs.webp`, `126-25-Colour-Tumblers-SS-Assorted.webp`, and `different colors.jpg`)[cite: 11].
  * Listed core services: Custom Product Printing, Corporate & Event Gifts, Online Design Previewing, and Automated Payment Processing[cite: 11].

* **Enquiry Page (`enquiry.html`):**
  * Built custom order enquiry form allowing users to input contact details, select product types, specify quantities, and type custom text[cite: 9].
  * Added logo image upload field for corporate branding requests[cite: 9].

* **Contact Us Page (`contactus.html`):**
  * Included complete business contact details, email address (`support@sipandsack.co.za`), operating hours, and Motherwell location details[cite: 8].
  * Created a direct message contact form for customer inquiries[cite: 8].
  * Embedded an interactive Google Map centered on Motherwell, Gqeberha, Eastern Cape[cite: 8].

* **Navigation & Consistency:**
  * Added standard header navigation menu across all 5 HTML pages linking `home`, `services`, `enquiry`, `aboutus`, and `contactus`[cite: 7, 8, 9, 10, 11].
  * Included consistent website footers with copyright details and quick contact links[cite: 8, 9, 11].
